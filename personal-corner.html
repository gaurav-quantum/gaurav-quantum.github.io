05 / Gardening

A little room to grow.

What began with a few tomato plants on my balcony in 2023 has grown into a love of gardening. Watching a seed become a seedling, and then a thriving plant, brings a quiet sense of wonder. Along the way, the garden keeps teaching me patience, care, and the value of paying attention.

From seed to something green

My growing collection

I have grown tomatoes, Thai green chillies, Thai basil, bell peppers, and fennel from seed. Ornamental chillies, an orchid, and a lily have also found a place in my collection.

Tomatoes

Tomatoes were where it all began. Raising them from seed and watching the first flowers turn into fruit made gardening feel especially rewarding.

My main lessons: pay attention to soil moisture, give the plants a sunny spot, and provide support before fruit-laden stems become too heavy.

Thai green chillies

These taught me to be patient with germination and to pay closer attention to warmth and moisture. The wait makes those first seedlings all the more satisfying.

Thai basil

A favourite connection between my garden and kitchen. I have enjoyed growing Thai basil from seed and learning how light, watering, and pruning shape its growth.

Bell peppers, fennel, and more

More notes and photographs from my growing collection will appear here as I document what works, what surprises me, and what I would try differently.
